Dr. Tanaka co-organized the 1st Workshop on Emerging Architectures for Sensing, Computing, and Control, held at Purdue University on July 20–21, 2026.
The workshop was co-organized with Dr. Imraan Faruque, Dr. Hritom Das, and Dr. Timothy Brown, all from Oklahoma State University.
